Reporting a problem

Choose Copy diagnostics in Strum's menu and paste the result into your email. It helps a lot, and it leaves out device names, serial numbers and home folder paths. If you'd rather, you can also open a report on GitHub, where it's public.

If the pointer or keys get stuck, press Control-Option-Command-Escape first. macOS takes them back straight away.

Your license key

Your receipt email from Polar has a link to your key. Lost it? Log in to Polar's customer portal with the email address you bought with, and it's there. That's also where you remove a Mac you no longer use.

Refunds

If Strum doesn't work properly on your Mac and we can't fix it, you'll get your money back. Tell us what's going wrong. The terms have the details.
