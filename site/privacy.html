Privacy

Strum has no analytics and no crash reports. It only goes online for your licence and, if you want them, updates.

What goes online

  • Your licence. During the trial Strum sends nothing. When you enter a licence key, it sends the key to Polar, the shop that sells Strum, with the kind of Mac it's on (for example "MacBook Pro (14-inch, 2021)"), so you can tell your Macs apart in your Polar account. After that it checks the licence about once a week, sending the key and this Mac's activation number. Never the computer's name, your name or anything about how you use Strum.
  • Updates, only if you turn on update checks in Settings › General. Strum then asks GitHub for the newest version once a day.

Buying

Polar takes the payment and keeps your email address and receipt, under its own privacy policy. We see your order in Polar: your email address, what you bought, your country and your licence key. We use it to help you and to send refunds, nothing else.

What Strum reads

  • Touch positions and pressure from your trackpads. These stay in memory.
  • Which app is in front, so gestures can work per app.
  • With Accessibility, the window or control under the pointer, to move windows and for pointer haptics.

What it keeps

Everything stays on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Strum:

  • your gestures and settings (config.json);
  • the history of changes;
  • a status file;
  • pointer recordings (traces/), only if you run the pointer test or start a recording. They hold positions, sizes and times, nothing else, and only the newest 20 are kept;
  • your licence key and the date your trial started (licence.json);
  • what Polar last said about your licence (licence-evidence.json), so a refund or a removed Mac is remembered.

The trial's start date is also kept in Strum's preferences. Strum never logs or stores device names, Bluetooth addresses or serial numbers.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics you copy for a bug report contain the Strum and macOS versions, your Mac's model, the kinds and sizes of your trackpads, your settings and an outline of your gestures. Scripts, URLs, app names and home folder paths are left out. You decide where to send them.

AI agents

Agents can only reach your settings through the strum tool you connect them to. Scripts, Shortcuts and similar actions they add won't run until you approve them in Strum.

This website

The site has no cookies, analytics or trackers. The Buy button takes you to Polar's checkout.
