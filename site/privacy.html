Privacy

Strum never connects to the internet. There are no analytics and no crash reports.

What it reads

  • Touch positions and pressure from your trackpads. These stay in memory.
  • Which app is in front, so gestures can work per app.
  • With Accessibility, the window or control under the pointer, to move windows and for pointer haptics.

What it keeps

Everything stays on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Strum:

  • your gestures and settings (config.json);
  • the history of changes;
  • a status file;
  • pointer recordings (traces/), only if you run the pointer test or start a recording. They hold positions, sizes and times, nothing else.

Strum never logs or stores device names, Bluetooth addresses or serial numbers.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics you copy for a bug report contain the Strum and macOS versions, your Mac's model, the kinds and sizes of your trackpads, your settings and an outline of your gestures. Scripts, URLs, app names and home folder paths are left out. You decide where to paste them.

AI agents

Agents can only reach your settings through the strum tool you connect them to. Scripts, Shortcuts and similar actions they add won't run until you approve them in Strum.

This website

The site has no cookies, analytics or trackers.
