Thanks for buying Strum

Your license key is on its way by email. Here's how to add it.

  1. Open the receipt email from Polar and follow the link to your license key. Copy the key.
  2. In Strum, open the menu bar menu and choose Enter License, or go to Settings › General › License.
  3. Paste the key and click Activate. That's it, your gestures stay on.

One key works on 3 of your Macs, and every future update is included.
